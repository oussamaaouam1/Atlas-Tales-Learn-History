App icon artwork for "Atlas Tales", a family app of true history stories on an illustrated world map.

bold navy outlines) peeking happily over the top of a colourful illustrated globe (blue ocean, simple
green/yellow/orange continents with no borders), with a tiny white paper boat on the globe's ocean.
Background: warm sunny yellow (#FFC83D) to soft tangerine radial gradient, filling the whole square.
Style: bright modern flat storybook illustration, soft shading, bold rounded navy outlines (#14213D).
Constraints: square 1024x1024, full-bleed background (no transparency, no rounded corners - the stores add
the mask), subject centred inside the middle 80%, no text, no letters, no logos.
